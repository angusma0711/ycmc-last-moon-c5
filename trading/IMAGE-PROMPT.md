## Generation prompt

Use case: scientific-educational / illustration-story.
Asset type: a six-panel wordless illustration sprite sheet for a Hong Kong primary-school classroom web lesson about shares.
Make ONE clean landscape sheet in a precise 3-column by 2-row grid, six equally sized SQUARE panels, no borders or gutters. Overall aspect ratio 3:2. Every panel has an identical pale warm ivory background (#f7f5ef) and generous 10% safe margins so each panel can be displayed separately by CSS cropping. Polished editorial storybook illustration with simple rounded shapes, subtle paper texture, deep navy outlines, muted teal, honey gold and coral. Suitable for P4-P6 pupils, visually clear from a projector. Keep the same two friendly fictional human characters and the same teal share certificate across the story. These are educational illustrations, not a broker app.
Panel order left-to-right, top-to-bottom:
1. A small friendly fictional bakery business with a croissant emblem, and a teal ownership/share certificate in the foreground. The certificate shows a tiny bakery icon, no writing. This communicates owning a small part of a business.
2. Two fictional people exchanging a gold coin pouch and the teal share certificate. Coins move in one direction, certificate in the opposite direction, with two clear broad directional arrows. Neither person is the bakery. Balanced trading, no celebration.
3. The same certificate in the centre, with two alternative paths: a teal upward arrow on one side and a coral downward arrow on the other. Both possibilities equally prominent, no promise of profit.
4. A coin wallet beside one teal share certificate, together on a simple tray. Coins and ownership are distinct assets; no arrows implying free money.
5. The two people exchanging the certificate back for a coin pouch, opposite roles to panel 2, with clear arrows. A tiny separate coin is set aside as a fee; no numbers.
6. Three distinct fictional game objects neatly arranged: a blue crystal, a golden bell and a red potion bottle, plus a simple paper trade card and pencil. Do not show any product-price rules, trends or rising arrows here.
TEXT: none. No letters, words, numerals, currency signs, watermarks, legends or labels anywhere. All labels and exact numbers will be added by the application in accessible HTML. Do not merge panels, cross panel boundaries, add extra panels or frame the whole sheet. Avoid real company branding, casinos, luxury wealth, trading terminals, stock predictions or guaranteed gains.
